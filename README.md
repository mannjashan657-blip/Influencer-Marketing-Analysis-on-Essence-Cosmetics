## 🎯 Project Overview

Essence Cosmetics partners with multiple beauty/lifestyle influencers to promote its product line. This dashboard consolidates campaign data — revenue, profit, follower counts, and engagement metrics — into a single view that lets stakeholders quickly answer:

- Which influencers generate the most **revenue** and **profit**?
- How does **follower count** correlate with **profitability**?
- Who drives the most **engagement** (likes, comments, shares)?
- What is each influencer's **share of total sales**?

The goal is to help the marketing team **reallocate budget toward the highest-ROI partnerships**.

## 📌 Dashboard Components

| Visual | Insight Delivered |
|---|---|
| **Revenue by Influencer** | Ranks influencers by total revenue generated |
| **Revenue vs Profit by Influencer** | Compares top-line revenue against actual profit per influencer |
| **Estimated Sales Contribution** | Donut chart showing each influencer's % share of total sales |
| **Followers vs Profit** | Scatter plot testing whether a larger following translates to higher profit |
| **Likes, Comments & Estimated Shares** | Breaks down engagement volume by influencer |

---

## 💡 Key Insights

- **Sofia Camara** is the top performer across nearly every metric — highest revenue (**$42K**), highest profit contribution, largest sales share (**31.91%**), and the most engagement (**48K likes**).
- **Holly Roche** ranks second in both revenue (**$31K**) and sales contribution (**23.4%**), making her a strong secondary partnership.
- The **Followers vs Profit** scatter plot shows profit does **not scale linearly with follower count** — some mid-sized influencers generate disproportionately high profit relative to their audience size, suggesting engagement quality matters more than raw reach.
- **Glam by Jordan** and **Lexi** show comparatively lower revenue and engagement, flagging them as candidates for either optimized content strategy or budget reallocation.
